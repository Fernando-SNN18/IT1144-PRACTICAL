The University of Vavuniya, established in 2021 as Sri Lanka's 17th state university, is a prominent public higher education institution located in the Northern Province. It offers a variety of degree programs across three faculties: Applied Science, Business Studies, and Technological Studies.Situated on an expansive 160-acre campus, the university aims to foster academic excellence, innovative research, and regional development. It traces its roots back to 1997 as the Vavuniya Campus of the University of Jaffna before being elevated to an independent university. Today, it provides comprehensive education while promoting social harmony and cultural diversity among its student body.You can explore more about their academic programs, admissions, and facilities on the official University of Vavuniya website.
